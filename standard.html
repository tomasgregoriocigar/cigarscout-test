The Standard

A published, evidence-based standard that rates online cigar retailers on how their actual customers experience them — and a seal only retailers who meet it may carry. Three commitments govern everything below: scores are never for sale; the evidence is published; the bar is the same for everyone, including our partners. Especially our partners.

Why this standard exists — two goals: to improve consumers' experience of buying cigars online, and to help retailers improve what they provide those consumers. Every retailer rated under it receives its scorecard — its areas of improvement and the path up — and when a retailer improves, that is published here too.

The score — 100 points, five pillars

25

Product as described

Freshness, condition on arrival, and whether what ships matches the listing.

25

Checkout honesty

No surprise programs, add-ons, or recurring charges. The total you see is the total you pay.

20

Shipping & packaging

Humidified, protected packaging, and delivery at the speed promised.

20

Service & resolution

Reachability, complaint handling, and whether problems get made right.

10

Would buy again

Repeat-purchase intent in consumer ratings and reviews.

Evidence is weighted: verified-purchase ratings from our own users first (collected after delivery, never supplied by the retailer), then pooled public consumer ratings and complaint records, then enthusiast-press and community consensus. The sample size behind every score is published. Below a minimum evidence floor, a retailer is Not Yet Rated — limited data, never scored on a whisper. Sudden bursts of ratings are flagged and held out pending review.

The bar and the tiers

Approved

Meets the standard

A score of 70 or higher, no pillar below 50, and no unresolved surprise-billing pattern. Approved retailers may license the TrustedCigar seal and are eligible for partnership and buy links.

Best

Top of the class

Each January, the highest-scoring Approved retailers (85+) earn the annual TrustedCigar Best designation for that year.

Below the bar

Listed, with the path up

Still published — score, most challenged scores, and exactly what qualifying takes. Not linked as a place to buy.

The automatic Do Not Recommend

An unresolved pattern of surprise recurring charges — a checkout add-on or "rewards program" that bills consumers monthly without unmistakable, separate, affirmative consent — places a retailer at Do Not Recommend regardless of its total score, until the pattern stops and affected consumers are made whole. Cadence, corrections, and the seal

Scores refresh as new evidence lands; formal re-scores publish quarterly with a dated change log. Every retailer receives its own scorecard — its score, its most challenged scores, and what qualifying takes — whether or not it ever speaks to us. Factual corrections are accepted at any time and, when verified, made and logged. The seal certifies the retailer, never a cigar; it is licensed only while a retailer remains Approved, and misuse or falling below the bar ends the license. Retailers pay for detailed scorecards and benchmark reports — the analysis, never the outcome.
