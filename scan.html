The scanner — paused while its catalogue is rebuilt

A scanner that answers "unknown" for most cigars isn't a tool — it's a tease. The TrustedCigar scanner launched knowing only a handful of barcodes, so we've paused it rather than let it guess.

It reopens when the catalogue behind it is real: verified barcodes, verified per-stick prices, only from retailers that meet the standard. When it returns, scanning the barcode on a box or cello — the band photo if the barcode won't read — will give you today's price range and a plain verdict: good price, fair, or walk away.

What's live now. The Retailer Trust Index — 16 retailers scored on published evidence — the Standard behind the scores, and the $14.95 charge: the billing surprise hiding in some checkouts, documented. Scores are never for sale.
