How to read this index. A BBB grade measures how a company handles complaints, not product quality — we weigh it as service evidence, nothing more. Coupon-site ratings are softer evidence and are never the sole basis for a tier. Retailers can submit factual corrections at any time; corrections that check out are made and logged publicly.
